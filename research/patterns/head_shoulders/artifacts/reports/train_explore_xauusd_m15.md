## Why "explore" and not "train-and-promote"

The §6.3 sample-size gate requires `n_total ≥ 300` / `n_oos ≥ 100`; H&S
plugins deliberately keep a strict anti-noise detector (Agent 4 DoD), so
their true sample size on M15 is below the gate: **HS 151, IHS 208**.  Forcing
n ≥ 300 explodes §8.3 FP ~0.7 — a documented trade-off.  These models are
therefore registered `lifecycle_state: trained` (never `validated`) and must
not be assigned/promoted until decided otherwise — and, for FW/RW, until the
P1 findings (t2) settle because any detector-config change would change the
`config_hash` and invalidate the artifact.

## Findings / recommendations

1. **falling_wedge — the near-miss is closed by the meta-model.**
   Agent 4's recorded §6.3 FAIL is the LR-on-12-gate-features procedure
   (PR-AUC 0.733 vs required 0.737).  The trained tier-2 meta-model (RF +
   isotonic, 28 causal features) scores **OOS PR-AUC 0.778 > 0.737** on the
   same purge+embargo split — i.e. the rule+structure signal does clear the
   PR-AUC margin once a capable scorer is used.  Hand this to t2 as direct
   evidence for P1 finding #8 (falling_wedge PR-AUC 0.004 short).

2. **head_shoulders — weak signal at true sample size.**  OOS PR-AUC 0.402
   vs baseline 0.367 is effectively no edge; below gate on n, PF CI and
   stability (Agent 4 report).  Recommended: do **not** promote on M15; if
   H&S is pursued, the spec's own §11 hint — H&S is a D1-pattern — is the
   right next experiment.  Model stays `trained` for audit.

3. **inverse_head_shoulders — decent but underpowered.**  OOS PR-AUC 0.585
   vs baseline 0.422 / ROC 0.646 is real signal on paper, but n=208 sits
   below the sample-size gate and OOS PF CI lower (0.921) is a near-miss.
   Keep `trained`; re-evaluate after accumulating data or on a longer
   history.

4. **rising_wedge deliberately NOT trained here.**  RW is P1 finding #7
   (t2 scope): improving its detector would change `config_hash` and
   invalidate any artifact trained now.  Train an RW model only after t2
   freezes the runner config.
